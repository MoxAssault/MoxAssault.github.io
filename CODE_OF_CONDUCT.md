# Code of Conduct

[Overview](README.md) | [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) | [Security](SECURITY.md) | **Code of Conduct** | [License](LICENSE)

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and honest when using the site, opening issues, reviewing changes, or contributing code and documentation.

Do not use project information to harass others, bypass community rules, misrepresent authorship, distribute harmful content, or facilitate unlawful activity.

No part of this repository, its files, its documentation, or the hosted site should be repackaged, sold, paywalled, or otherwise used for monetary gain.

Report security concerns through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a public disclosure containing sensitive details.

Maintainers may remove contributions, comments, or participation that violate these expectations. This conduct policy does not alter the separate licenses that apply to bundled third-party software.
